Employee Handbook

Employees are expected to follow the organization code of conduct, protect company information, and maintain respectful communication with colleagues, customers, and partners.

Standard working hours are 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM, Monday through Friday. Flexible work arrangements may be approved by the reporting manager based on project requirements and business continuity needs.

Employees must complete mandatory onboarding training within 15 days of joining. Mandatory modules include information security awareness, HR policies, workplace ethics, and data privacy.

Performance reviews are conducted twice per year. Employees and managers should document goals, achievements, skill development areas, and feedback before the review discussion.

Business travel expenses must be submitted within 10 working days after travel completion with valid invoices and manager approval.
