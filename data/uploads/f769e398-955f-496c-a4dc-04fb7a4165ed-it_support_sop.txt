IT Support SOP

All IT support requests must be submitted through the Service Desk portal. Employees should include the asset tag, operating system, error screenshot, business impact, and contact number.

Priority 1 incidents include complete network outages, production system downtime, security lockouts affecting multiple users, and data loss events. Priority 1 incidents must be acknowledged within 15 minutes and escalated to the IT Operations Manager.

Password reset requests are treated as standard service requests. The helpdesk must verify the employee identity using the registered mobile number or manager approval before resetting credentials.

Laptops issued to employees must have endpoint protection, disk encryption, VPN configuration, and approved productivity software installed before handover.

Resolved tickets must include root cause, action taken, resolution time, and preventive recommendation before closure.
