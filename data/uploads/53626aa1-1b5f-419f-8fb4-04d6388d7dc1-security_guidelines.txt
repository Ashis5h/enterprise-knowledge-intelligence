Enterprise Security Guidelines

Employees must use multi-factor authentication for email, VPN, cloud applications, and administrative systems. MFA must not be disabled without written approval from the Information Security team.

Confidential documents must not be shared through personal email, public file-sharing links, or unmanaged messaging applications. Approved channels include the enterprise document portal and encrypted corporate email.

Any suspected phishing email must be reported using the phishing report button in the mail client. Employees should not click links, download attachments, or reply to suspicious messages.

USB storage devices are restricted by default. Temporary USB access requires manager approval, business justification, and endpoint security review.

Security incidents must be reported within 30 minutes of discovery. Examples include lost devices, unauthorized access, malware alerts, credential exposure, and accidental data disclosure.
