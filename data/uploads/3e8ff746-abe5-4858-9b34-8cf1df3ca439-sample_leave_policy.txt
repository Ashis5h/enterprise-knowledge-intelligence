Enterprise Leave Policy

Employees are entitled to 12 casual leaves per calendar year. Casual leave must be requested through the HR portal at least one working day in advance except during emergencies.

Employees are entitled to 15 earned leaves per calendar year. Earned leave requests of more than five consecutive working days require manager approval and HR acknowledgement.

Employees are entitled to 10 sick leaves per calendar year. Sick leave longer than two consecutive working days requires a medical certificate.

Unused casual leave does not carry forward to the next year. Unused earned leave can be carried forward up to a maximum balance of 30 days.
